## What scenario QL holds

The lane starts a real bounded Fogell-host approval, waits for the pending prompt, and substitutes a FIFO only after publication. That FIFO is a test-only slow-storage surrogate: the host enters the read while the deadline is live, receives one exact approval after expiry, and then reaches the mandatory post-poll cancellation check.

The lane requires all of the following:

- the host exits without hanging or reporting success and reports the build aborted;
- the pre-gate marker exists exactly once, the guarded post-gate marker is absent, and there are no extra effects;
- one exact `input-prompt-cancellable`, one exact `input-answer-provisional` for `approved` by `late-reader`, and one exact engine-written `input-decision-voided` occur globally and in that physical order;
- no actionable `input-decision` is written, and the approval inbox is empty after cancellation;
- a reconstructed crash snapshot keeps the bounded Jenkinsfile, provisional answer and void while removing later terminal records; replay exits `3` as `needs-reconciliation`, does not change the journal hash, repeats no effect, and publishes no inbox file.

The change is confined to `scripts/run-approval-lane.sh`. Scenario P remains the hand-written-void control; QL is the engine-emission case it could not supply.

## Evidence boundary

The provisional answer already makes the reconstructed state unsafe to replay. The engine-written void records audit truth; QL does **not** claim the void is the sole replay barrier.

The FIFO is not a supported approval-inbox spelling. It deterministically drives a late-return branch without relying on scheduler timing. This is a Fogell-host regression lane, not Jenkins differential evidence, and it does not reproduce the separate case where a faulting sibling races a late approval. The lane establishes the exact bounded scenario above; it does not generalize that result to every cancellation source or external storage implementation.
