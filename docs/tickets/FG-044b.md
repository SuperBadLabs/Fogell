# FG-044b — Credential and stash residual closure

Status: **PARTIAL** on 2026-08-30. The bounded candidate implementation,

## Bounded claim

This ticket closes the four findings recorded after PR #15:

1. `echo` interpolates ordinary GStrings, preserves single-quoted literals, and
   routes secret-bearing rendered text through masking.
2. A Fogell-generated `X_FILE` companion for an inner text credential never
   overwrites an explicit `X_FILE` supplied by an enclosing environment or
   credential scope.
3. The three supported credential descriptors (`string`, `file`, and
   `usernamePassword`) consume their complete call/list source. Quoted/commented
   decoys, trailing input, malformed segments, triple-quoted literals, physical newlines in ordinary
   quoted literals, duplicate keys, interpolated
   GStrings, escaped source values, nonliteral values, unknown keys, and suffix-shaped keys cannot
   disappear beside a supported request. Unsupported
   input fails the whole wrapper before its body; known descriptors retain Jenkins'
   unknown-parameter warning metadata. Whole-request structural damage is diagnosed
   as malformed syntax rather than a fictitious binding kind. Token trivia is
   restricted to Groovy's exact ASCII whitespace set; .NET-only Unicode whitespace
   such as NBSP is never erased into a supported request.
4. Stash applies Ant 1.10.17's 28 case-sensitive default excludes unless the exact
   `useDefaultExcludes: false` opt-out is supplied.

This is not a claim that Fogell evaluates arbitrary Jenkins credential expressions.
The supported boundary is deliberately narrower: escape-free quoted literal
arguments for three descriptor kinds, with everything else refused before
credential effects.

## Non-goals and residuals

This closure does not claim:

- arbitrary Groovy evaluation inside credential descriptor arguments;
- same-UID secret isolation, owned by FG-070b;
- generic stash workspace containment or file/directory-symlink parity;
- platform-specific Ant separator behavior or runtime mutation of Ant defaults; or
- archive/JUnit selection behavior beyond their separately ticketed contracts.

Stash symlink behavior is isolated as FG-228. Splitting it is load-bearing: this
ticket says exactly what the receipts measured and cannot be cited as proof that
stash refuses or safely follows links to external targets.
