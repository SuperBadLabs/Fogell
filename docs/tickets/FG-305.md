# FG-305 — Make burst output safe and usable

**Wave:** W2 · **Priority:** P0 · **State:** Done

The September 26 default-traced 350-marker workload filled the 1,024-callback
queue and failed well below the whole-build byte/record limits. Change the
transport so a normal burst can drain through bounded memory without losing
or reordering output. Do not treat a larger whole-build limit as the fix.

The first design candidate is bounded producer backpressure on the existing
serialized callback queue. Any waiter must wake on capacity release and on
queue close/failure; a stalled callback must not make process cleanup unbounded.

**Done when:** the exact 350-marker reproducer succeeds repeatedly with every
marker once and in order across feedback pages; a workload above a documented
hard limit still fails closed with `OUTPUT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and readable evidence;
the native gate remains green. Record the chosen queue/backpressure bound.

**Closed 2026-09-29:** `016ed3b2`. The queue retains its 1,024-entry and 16 Mi
character limits. Producers wait up to five seconds for space; a stalled sink
fails closed and cleanup completes. The [qualification report](../../reports/native-output-2026-09-29/REPORT.md)
records the 15-page real-controller proof, 584/584 gate, and 115/115 matrix,
including the retained typed over-limit failure.
