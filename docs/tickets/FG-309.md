# FG-309 — Alert on held work and capacity

**Wave:** W3 · **Priority:** P1 · **State:** Ready

Surface reconciliation-required attempts, held retention journals, failed or
old backups, and filesystem/database capacity pressure from read-only checks.
Choose and record thresholds against the pilot host's measured capacity; do
not infer safety from a green controller health endpoint alone.

**Done when:** each alert can be induced safely in disposable state, reaches
the chosen operator channel once with a clear action, and clears only after the
condition is resolved. Record the thresholds, delivery path, and false-positive
observations in the staging report.
