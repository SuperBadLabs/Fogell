# FG-269 — Recovery and sustained self-hosted feedback pilot

Status: DONE. Milestone: M3. Depends on: FG-266, FG-267, FG-268.
Completion scope: local implementation and measured acceptance (2026-09-23); publication remains separate.

Assigned to the primary agent. The [versioned pilot profile](../runbooks/self-hosted-pilot.md)
declares the workload and recovery objectives before measurement. Implementation
and live acceptance are complete; the complete configured repository gate passed.

Problem: short fixture runs do not establish sustained useful operation or
recovery of a controller and its external state.

Scope: select a supported Fogell build/test pipeline; version its supported
profile; run paired database/state backup/restore and interrupted upgrade drills;
then a declared sustained load/failure campaign on the trusted single-writer host.

Acceptance: predeclare exact workload, hardware, versions, limits, concurrency,
duration, pass thresholds and recovery objectives; collect at least 30 loops per
reported warm/cold condition; report p50/p95 alongside failures and censored runs;
confirm tested source identities, expected test/artifact output, bounded storage,
cancellation and restart behavior. Restore rejects stale authority and reports
measured recovery time and data loss against the declared objectives.

Keep full required pre-publication checks; a fast development subset is labelled
as such. Release needs this evidence plus the production gates, not a Jenkins
corpus percentage.

  warm locked NuGet cache; loop p50/p95 8.754/9.693 seconds, typed diagnostic
  p50/p95 4.120/4.641 seconds. All identities and artifacts verified.
