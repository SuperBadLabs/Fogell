# FG-314 — Close the sustained native pilot

**Wave:** W4 · **Priority:** P1 · **State:** Ready · **Depends on:** FG-312, FG-313

Run at least seven consecutive days and 50 builds across Fogell, Maven, and
clenkins on the supported single-host profile. Monitor the W3 maintenance and
alert schedule throughout. Count every admission, including failed runs,
interrupted work, and retries. Measure accepted-submission-to-first-feedback
latency on an otherwise idle host against the five-second p95 working target.

**Done when:** the raw seven-day inventory reconciles with terminal and
reconciliation-required outcomes, artifacts and feedback remain retrievable,
the latency and resource distributions include every eligible sample, and the
report makes a pass/fail judgment for every M4 gate. Open defects for unexplained
or missing evidence; do not censor them from the pilot count.
