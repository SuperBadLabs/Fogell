# FG-310 — Prove unattended staging and cleanup catch-up

**Wave:** W3 · **Priority:** P1 · **State:** Ready · **Depends on:** FG-308, FG-309

Run the supported native controller for 72 hours with scheduled backups,
retention, and alerts. Include a planned maintenance interruption and prove
that pending cleanup resumes afterward without erasing held or uncertain work.
Keep the staging state separate from the existing pilot service.

**Done when:** raw schedule logs, recovery-point hashes, alert deliveries,
capacity measurements, and before/after cleanup inventories show three days
without silent skipped maintenance or unexplained evidence loss. Any failure
becomes a ticket before W3 closes.
