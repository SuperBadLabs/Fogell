## Implemented law

`RetryDecision.decide` accepts a terminal immutable parent attempt, a positive zero-based attempt limit, a proposed child identity and an optional prior decision.

- Below the limit, a fresh decision creates one queued child with the parent's organization, node and restore epoch, ordinal exactly one greater than the parent, `RetryOf = Some parent.Id`, the initial fence, and no lease owner or expiry.
- At or beyond the limit, it returns `BudgetExhausted`. No child exists on that route, so the proposed child ID is deliberately neither validated nor consumed.
- A prior decision is returned exactly only after its captured parent organization, node, ordinal, ancestry and restore epoch, its original attempt limit, and its complete outcome are validated. A hostile current proposed ID or changed current limit cannot rewrite that prior result.
- Empty identities, active parents, invalid parent ordinals, non-positive limits, malformed or foreign prior decisions, and child IDs equal to the parent or its known direct ancestor are refused.
- Which terminal outcomes are retryable is caller policy and remains outside this law.

Scratch mutation checks made the principal budget, ancestry, lease/fence, active-parent and prior-validation branches load-bearing; those scratch copies are not retained evidence.
