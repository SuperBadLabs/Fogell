# FG-308 — Schedule paired backups and retention

**Wave:** W3 · **Priority:** P0 · **State:** Ready

Define an unattended maintenance schedule for matching PostgreSQL and complete
state-root recovery points, with recorded hashes and age. Keep writers quiesced
for a paired point as required by the recovery runbook. Only then schedule the
bounded retention sweep for explicitly selected organizations. Retention has no
dry run and may leave `Pending` work or a held journal; never treat exit 0 as
proof that all cleanup finished.

**Done when:** a staged schedule produces multiple verifiable recovery points
at an interval consistent with the 24-hour RPO; a restore of a selected point
is checked in disposable destinations; retention catches up without touching
active/uncertain work; and held or incomplete sweeps are surfaced to operators.
