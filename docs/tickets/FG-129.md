# FG-129 — The harness cannot seal a receipt for ANY compile-shaped refusal

Status: **PARTIAL — landed on 2026-08-24 and never accounted; re-verified on
2026-09-03 with two further compile-refusal receipts; exact-head hosted gate
and review coverage are outstanding** (P2).

## The limit, as filed

A Jenkins build refused at COMPILE time emits no `[Pipeline]` annotations,
and the trace classifier was deliberately gated on real structure, so a
compile-shaped refusal on the reference engine reduced to `Started by user
unknown or anonymous` against Fogell's empty output and could not seal a
PROVEN receipt even when result and workspace agreed. Every compile-shaped
refusal the engine implemented was therefore recorded "UNPROVEN by receipt
(FG-129)".

## What landed, unaccounted

Signed commit `d27616c5` ("FG-129 seal compile-refusal equivalence",
2026-08-24) made refusal a comparison AXIS before output shaping:

- `Jenkins.classifyExecutionDisposition` classifies a Jenkins build as
  `RefusedBeforeExecution` from a failure with zero `[Pipeline]` annotations
  and an ordered exact console envelope (the FG-129 test list pins the
  envelope rule and refuses spoofs), and an SCM-defined refusal is
  recognised without a Pipeline-start marker.
- `Compare.traces` compares dispositions first: a one-sided refusal is a
  divergence; when BOTH sides refused, neither executed user code, so
  compiler narration, timestamp coverage and diagnostic wording are outside
  runtime semantics and only disposition, terminal result and the workspace
  hash are compared and sealed (`sealed-output: omitted`; the receipt's
  contract block says so).
- Case and receipt `compile-refusal-invalid-eight` (FG-126a's measured `\8`
  spelling) sealed the first compile refusal PROVEN tier 1, and the test list
  `FG-129 compile refusals are comparable without becoming execution` pins
  the classifier and the comparison.

The commit reached `main` inside PR #148 (FG-224, merged 2026-08-27T03:55Z
as `20d4b638`) and this row stayed TODO, the same shape as FG-207, FG-123a
and FG-203 from the same branch. Fifteen comments in `src/` and `tests/`
kept saying a compile-shaped refusal "seals none", "emits nothing
comparable" or "cannot be receipted" after that date (thirteen found by the
sweep's author, two more — FG-174's in the Declarative parser — by the
pre-push verifier), and the FG-239 and FG-240 tickets repeated it on
2026-09-03.

## Re-verified, 2026-09-03

The two FG-239/FG-240 shapes were sealed on the pinned lab through the
mechanism above: `compile-refusal-timeout-unit-stage` and
`compile-refusal-timeout-unit-pipeline` are PROVEN tier 1 — same
pre-execution refusal on both engines, same result, same (empty) workspace
hash, output not compared. All 296 receipt seals verify (`--verify-seals`)
and the generated scorecard moves from 293 of 293 to 295 of 295 expected
cases. The FG-129 test list passes in the Differential project.

The fifteen stale comments now say "UNPROVEN by receipt (sealable since
FG-129 landed; none sealed for this shape)" where no receipt exists, or name
the receipt where one now does (the timeout-unit sites). The FG-239 and
FG-240 tickets' "no receipt obtainable" residuals are corrected to cite their
receipts, the FG-239 board row's copy of that sentence is corrected, and the
open FG-132 row's expectation that receipts "will hit the FG-129 limit" is
retracted in place; the closed FG-053, FG-121 and FG-174 rows keep their
historical wording with a dated supersession note.

## Residuals and non-claims

- Only the refusal disposition, terminal result and workspace hash are
  compared for a refused pair; the compiler's wording is deliberately outside
  the claim, so a receipt proves "both refused before executing", not "for
  the same reason".
- Every other compile-shaped refusal the engine implements (`timestamps`
  arguments and blocks, stage `retry` counts, `unstable` arity, duplicate
  sections, FG-132's duplicate `options`) remains UNPROVEN by receipt until
  a case is sealed for it; each is now sealable and each comment says so.
