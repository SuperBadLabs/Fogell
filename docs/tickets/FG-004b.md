## Implemented boundary

Fogell accepts untrusted Jenkinsfile text at its admission and parser boundary.
The fixed-seed FG-004b campaign sends exactly 10,000 unique inputs through the
same Declarative `Parser.parse` entry point used by callers. Every source is
guaranteed to be refused at that boundary: recipe families supply empty/trivia
input, a valid scripted source with no Declarative `pipeline` block, incomplete
Declarative structure, unclosed delimiters, or named admission-limit boundaries
for source bytes, node count, nesting depth and scalar length. A successful
Declarative parse is therefore a failure of the campaign, not another outcome
to classify.

`2029b027dceb43a63f66412a2b00413d45276386`, which is an ancestor of the
closure head. Its length-delimited input corpus is replay-pinned to SHA-256

## Scope and nonclaims

This is a deterministic, bounded admission-negative robustness campaign. It is
not a grammar-aware or coverage-guided fuzzer, does not claim to enumerate
arbitrary malformed Groovy or Jenkinsfile text, and makes no compatibility claim
against a Jenkins oracle. Some controls are valid scripted inputs and are
negative only at the Declarative boundary. The guarantee is narrower and
falsifiable: these exact 10,000 replay-pinned inputs must continue to return
typed, positioned refusals without escaping the Declarative parser boundary as
exceptions.

FG-004 owns the admission limits themselves. FG-004b implements the standing
campaign that continuously drives guaranteed-refused input through those
limits and the Declarative parser; it does not expand the configured limits or
parser grammar.
