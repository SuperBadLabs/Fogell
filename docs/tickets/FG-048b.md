# FG-048b — Remaining `when` conditions: `buildingTag`, `changeset`, `changelog`, `triggeredBy`, `changeRequest`, `isRestartedRun`

**Status:** **DONE**  ·  **Priority:** P1

Archived from the execution board on 2026-08-02. The board carries the
outcome; this file carries the reasoning, which is what a future reader
needs and what a planning table cannot hold.

---

33 corpus files use `when`; up to 15 mention one of these. **Coverage is now complete against Jenkins' own enumeration.** Rejecting a nested `beforeAgent` made Jenkins print the authoritative list — *"Valid conditionals are: allOf, anyOf, branch, buildingTag, changeRequest, changelog, changeset, environment, equals, expression, isRestartedRun, not, tag, triggeredBy"* — all **fourteen** of which are modelled. MEASURED on a plain job: all six context-dependent ones evaluate **false**, their stages are skipped, and the build **succeeds** — so modelling them turns a refusal into a working build. `when-context-conditions` receipt PROVEN tier-1. Three further parser gaps were found while measuring the result: `anyOf { a; b }` with **semicolon** separators degraded the whole condition to unmodelled (6 corpus occurrences); `beforeAgent true` is an evaluation-ORDER option, not a condition, and being unmodelled it failed the whole `when` closed; and one missing `_` in the operand charset left `equals actual: _deploy_to_nexus` unmodelled. **Declarative corpus files whose `when` conditions are now ALL modelled: 28 of 28, up from 24** — none fail closed. BOUNDARY: only the context-ABSENT case is receipt-proven; a real multibranch build with a `CHANGE_ID`, or one with an actual changelog, is not — FG-048c
